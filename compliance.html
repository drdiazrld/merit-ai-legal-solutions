Where Minnesota’s AI rules are going

Minnesota’s rules for AI in professional services are still moving. SF 4927, a bill before the Legislature on delivering professional services through AI directly to consumers, has not been enacted, and we do not claim compliance with any statute that has not taken effect.

What we build to today is the Minnesota Rules of Professional Conduct: you direct the work, you review every output, and you are the author. Current evidence supports only a related, narrower function; the broader wording is not certified and attorney verification remains required.
