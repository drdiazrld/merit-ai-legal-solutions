The category

The AI legal associate, for Minnesota practice

An AI legal associate carries the work of an associate: research, drafting, deadlines, and court operations. It is not a chatbot that answers questions, and it is not a research database. It produces work product that the attorney reviews, approves, and authors.

What it is

Work product, not answers

Ask a general assistant a legal question and you get prose you have to check yourself. Hand this associate a matter and you get the draft, the authorities it pulled, the deadline calendar computed from your own dates, and the document in eFile shape, all waiting for your review.

It is configured to your forms, your practice areas, and your jurisdiction. It runs one agent per attorney, isolated per matter.

What it is not

Not a law firm, not an oracle

  • Merit AI Solutions is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice
  • The attorney reviews and approves every output and remains the author
  • Calendaring is uncertified and in beta; the citation gate is a separate manual step, not integrated
  • SOC 2 and ISO 27001 are not held and are not claimed
