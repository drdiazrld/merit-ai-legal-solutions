__TITLE__ Capabilities — Merit AI Legal Solutions
Capabilities

Eight working systems. One agent.

Every system below is live, tested, and running under attorney review. No roadmaps — this is what the AI Legal Associate does today.

System A · Research

Full-matter research in minutes

Research pulls statutes, court rules, and real case law from a 357-authority Minnesota index — 20 statutes, 45 court rules, 291 real cases — organized around your matter's facts, not keywords. Intake completes in about 2.4 seconds, and every authority passes the citation gate before it appears in a draft.

  • Statutes, rules, and real cases — organized around your facts
  • Intake in ~2.4 seconds
  • Outside the index, it says NOT_FOUND — it doesn't guess
The law library
System B · Citation safety

A gate that refuses to invent

Every citation is re-verified live against official Minnesota sources and returned with one of three verdicts: VERIFIED, NOT_RETRIEVABLE, or AMBIGUOUS. Only VERIFIED citations ship — a fabricated case gets NOT_RETRIEVABLE and is excluded. A ten-citation batch verifies in seconds.

  • Live re-fetch at verification time — not "looks right"
  • Three verdicts: VERIFIED · NOT_RETRIEVABLE · AMBIGUOUS
  • Two cases sharing a page cite? It says AMBIGUOUS and asks you to choose
Checked against the shelf
System C · Drafting

Memos, motions, and filings in minutes

Drafting works from the record, not from memory: research memos, demand letters, motions, complaints, answers, interrogatories, and briefs, in the formats Minnesota courts expect. Every draft ships with its citation verdicts attached, so your review is fast and grounded.

  • Memos · demands · motions · complaints · answers · interrogatories · briefs
  • Citation verdicts attached to every draft
  • Nothing submits itself — drafts carry SUBMIT: None
From the record
System D · Case Brain

Every matter, one living file

Case Brain keeps parties, timeline, facts, evidence log, issues, and open tasks in one place, current as the matter grows. Evidence is logged with its source — nothing is asserted into the record without a source behind it.

  • Parties · timeline · facts · evidence · issues · tasks
  • Every assertion carries a source
  • Current as the matter grows — not a static folder
One file per matter
System E · Deadline engine

Seventeen verified Minnesota deadlines

The deadline engine computes from the Minnesota Rules of Civil Procedure — 17 verified deadlines, calculated from your matter's own dates. It answers what attorneys actually ask: what's due, when, and what happens if it's missed.

  • 17 verified Minnesota deadlines, calculated
  • Computed from your matter's dates — not a static calendar
  • Deadlines roll into your matter file automatically
What's due, and when
System F · Court operations

The courthouse, digitized

Court operations keeps 87 courthouse cards for Minnesota courts — locations, filing systems, and procedures — and prefills court forms like the CIV117 from your matter record. Filing goes through the courts' own e-filing portal: the agent prepares, the court's system files, you approve every step.

  • 87 courthouse cards for Minnesota courts
  • Form prefill — CIV117 and more
  • Filing through the courts' own e-filing portal
87 cards. One portal.
System G · Orchestration

Seven tracks, one brief

One request dispatches seven parallel workstreams — facts, research, evidence, weaknesses, opposing arguments, open tasks — then an adversarial QA pass tries to break the draft. What reaches you is the merged, challenged, corrected brief, in roughly 10–15 minutes before your review.

  • Seven parallel tracks from a single request
  • Adversarial QA challenges the merged draft
  • One brief in — not seven documents to reconcile
Seven tracks. One brief.
System H · Trust & privacy

Your files stay yours

One agent per attorney, isolated per matter. Only task-relevant content is used for each step, and your files are not used for training. Every action is logged; nothing auto-files or auto-submits, and attorney approval is the last step of every workflow.

  • Per-attorney isolation; task-relevant content only
  • No training on your files
  • Audit log of every action · approval before anything ships
Your files stay where they live
