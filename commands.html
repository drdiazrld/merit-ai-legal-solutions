How commands work

  1. Choose the matterWork stays tied to the matter the attorney intentionally opens.
  2. State one taskUse a plain-English request. Provide the available facts, records, dates, and instructions.
  3. Review the draftCheck every fact, authority, citation, date, calculation, and document before use.
  4. Take any external action manuallyThe attorney sends, files, signs, serves, mails, or submits. The command does not.

Representative commands

This page shows representative requests. Availability is confirmed for the selected workflow, and every result remains subject to attorney review.

Matter summary

  • Summarize the current matter record and list open questions.
  • Prepare a concise status summary for attorney review.

Research

  • Research the stated issue and identify sources for attorney review.
  • Identify what additional current-law research is required.

Statutes and rules

  • Locate the governing statute or rule and quote the relevant text.
  • Compare the supplied rule text with the matter facts.

Citation checking

  • Check whether each citation exists and supports the stated proposition.
  • List citations that still require attorney review for currency or treatment.

Facts and chronology

  • Build a chronology from the supplied matter records.
  • Separate supported facts, disputed facts, and missing facts.

Document review

  • Summarize this document and identify provisions requiring attorney attention.
  • Compare these documents and list material differences.

Drafting

  • Draft the communication for attorney review.
  • Prepare a draft from the supplied records and instructions.

Preparation

  • Prepare an attorney review checklist for the upcoming event.
  • Organize the supplied materials into a preparation outline.

Matter maintenance

  • List incomplete matter information and records that require attention.
  • Prepare a matter-status update from the current record.

Deadlines and tasks

  • Calculate the deadline and show the complete rule-and-date analysis.
  • Prepare an internal task list and identify which dates require verification.

Filing and forms

  • Prepare the filing materials and identify what remains for attorney review.
  • Populate the supplied form with known matter information and leave unknowns blank.

Command boundaries

  • No emailing, mailing, or sending.
  • No filing, signing, serving, or submitting.
  • Every deadline requires independent attorney verification.
  • Citation retrieval or checking is not a complete citator, current-validity, or subsequent-history result.
  • Missing facts remain missing; the system must not invent them.
  • Internal tasks or calendars do not prove an entry exists in an external calendar.
  • Every output remains a draft until attorney review.
