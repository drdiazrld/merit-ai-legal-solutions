Security & Privacy

How secure are we — compared to others.

On what matters most for client confidences, we're designed to be stronger out of the box than general-purpose AI and on par with the big legal platforms' best defaults — with one honest gap: formal certifications.

Where the design is strong

This is designed in, not promised

Six properties of the architecture — not settings you have to remember to turn on.

One agent per attorney

Your matters are isolated from every other attorney's. Nothing cross-connects, by design.

Files stay where they live

The system works against the files you already use — it doesn't require uploading your whole file library into a third-party cloud. That's a structural difference, not a setting.

Only what the task needs

No training on your files

Your content is not ingested for model training.

Nothing submits itself

The plain-language version

Your files stay where they live.

Every client file you work on — pleadings, discovery, correspondence, the whole record — stays exactly where it already is: in your office, on your computer, in your systems. The Associate doesn't take your files anywhere.

Here's how that works. The Associate is a working assistant that comes to your files — not a service your files are sent to. When you give it a task, it reads what that task needs, from the files where they already sit, and works from what it read. It doesn't pull your file into a separate system. It doesn't store copies on outside servers. It doesn't keep your documents after the task is done.

1

The work comes to the file, not the other way around.

The Associate reaches into your record in place, the way a colleague would pull a folder from your filing cabinet. The folder stays in the cabinet.

2

Only what the task needs, only for that task.

Each request touches only the documents that request calls for — not your whole file. What it reads is used for that task and that task alone. It isn't kept to train anything, and it isn't added to anyone else's work.

3

Nothing moves on its own.

The Associate can't submit, file, email, or send anything. If a filing needs to happen, it happens the way it normally does — you do it, in the court's own filing system, under your name and your signature.

And because the work happens where the files live, there's a record of it: every step the Associate takes in your matters is logged, and you can see what it did and why. You stay in control of the record, the calendar, and every document that goes out.

That's the whole design: your files stay where they live, and no files leave the system.

Compared to others

Honest comparisons, no names needed

vs. general-purpose AI chatbots

Out of the box they're the weakest for client confidences — prompts can become training data, chat history isn't per-firm isolated, and they'll happily draft (and invent) from memory.

The enterprise versions of the big general tools can be configured with no-retention and no-training — that's real, and we won't pretend otherwise — but they still don't verify citations, and they're not built around per-attorney isolation.

vs. big legal AI platforms

They have formal security programs — SOC 2-type attestations, compliance teams, enterprise procurement reviews. That's genuinely more paperwork-certified than we are, and we won't claim parity there.

But their model is the opposite of ours: firm-wide, cloud-heavy, seat-based — your files go into their platform. We're built single-attorney, files-in-place, nothing auto-submits.

The honest bottom line: confidentiality defaults — ahead of consumer AI, comparable to enterprise legal AI. Formal certifications — that's the gap, if a firm ever requires SOC 2-type attestation on paper.

The citation gate

A security control for the record

Confidentiality is one kind of security. Integrity is another: the failure attorneys fear most is the invented citation — a case that doesn't exist, cited with total confidence.

The gate returns one of three verdicts — VERIFIED, NOT_RETRIEVABLE, or AMBIGUOUS — and only VERIFIED ships in a draft. A fabricated case in our test set was refused and excluded. That's the design, tested.
