Before the pilot starts

The discovery call and workflow review determine whether the task fits the current service. They also identify requirements for confidentiality, access, records, review, and support.

Use only fictional information in demonstrations. Any later use of client information requires separately documented approval and review.

During the pilot

Merit prepares the agreed material. The attorney checks it and decides whether it can be used.

Citation checking is separate from drafting. Proposed deadlines require independent verification. The attorney manually files, sends, serves, or submits final work.

Measure the defined review work

  • Did the output reflect the supplied records?
  • Were open or uncertain facts easy to identify?
  • Could the attorney trace research statements to sources?
  • How much attorney review and revision did the task require?
  • Did the workflow fit the firm’s supervision process?
