What a hire costs beyond the salary

The salary is the visible line. The rest is recruiting and the weeks spent reading resumes, onboarding, training on your own forms and your courthouse, benefits and payroll taxes, a workstation and software seats, and the months before the person carries a full load.

When the person leaves, you pay that again. The knowledge of how your firm drafts its motions and where your files live leaves with them.
