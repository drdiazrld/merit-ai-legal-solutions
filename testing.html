Scope limits

Component and path specific

These results apply to the specific citation checks, tested paths, and targeted probes described above. They do not establish broad legal accuracy, deadline accuracy, or client outcomes.

Separate from drafting

Citation checking is a separate step. Merit does not claim that every drafting or other workflow automatically runs the citation check.

Attorney verification remains required

The attorney opens and evaluates sources, checks proposition support and application, independently verifies every proposed deadline, and decides what to use.

What each test report includes

Each Merit test report identifies the details readers need to understand the result:

  • Test date
  • Software revision or release
  • Number of test items and runs
  • Inputs and expected outcomes
  • Observed result for the stated test
  • Whether attorney-review time is included
  • Whether the test covers a component or a complete workflow
  • Current disposition only when a dated retest supports it

A timing result appears with its corresponding correctness result. Merit is compared with manual work only when both conditions use a defined, comparable method.

What these tests do and do not show

The public results support only the components and paths named above. No claim is made here about monthly savings, every document type, complete source coverage, automatic citation checking in every workflow, broad legal or deadline accuracy, or client outcomes.
