Testing & Accuracy

The most accurate AI — confidence in the data and the workflow it provides.

Not "never wrong" — no one can promise that honestly. Instead: a hard gate, real tests, and an attorney in the loop. Here is exactly how we test.

The 357-authority Minnesota index
How every answer is tested

Three layers of testing

Real sources only

The agent works from official Minnesota statutes and court rules and public case records — a 357-authority index. Nothing is written from memory.

Live verification

Every citation is re-fetched at verification time. A citation is good because it was just checked — not because it looks right.

A gate, not a hope

Every citation receives one of three verdicts — VERIFIED, NOT_RETRIEVABLE, or AMBIGUOUS — and only VERIFIED ships in a draft.

The anti-fabrication test

We test the failure that matters most

The failure attorneys fear most is the invented citation — a case that doesn't exist, cited with total confidence. So we test exactly that.

Our test set inserts fabricated cases and statutes into real requests. The agent must refuse them, flag them, and keep them out of the draft.

  • A fabricated case in the test set was returned NOT_RETRIEVABLE and excluded from the draft
  • A real Minnesota statute in the same test verified live
  • Every drafting run in the suites ends with SUBMIT: None — nothing self-submits

Honesty

What we won't claim

  • We won't claim "never." Anyone who promises you an AI that can't make a mistake is selling something.
  • We won't dress up an unverified citation. If it can't be verified, it's flagged or refused — that's the design, not an accident.
  • When two real cases share the same citation page, the gate says AMBIGUOUS and asks you to choose the controlling one. It doesn't guess for you.
  • When a question falls outside our index, it says NOT_FOUND. It does not invent an answer to look helpful.

What we do claim: the most accurate AI for Minnesota legal work — the one that gives you confidence in the data and the workflow it provides, under your review.
