The work, and the time

Everything it handles, and what that saves you

The AI Legal Associate does the paralegal and legal-assistant work that consumes your support staff, and does it in minutes. Every task below is live and tested. In every case, the attorney reviews and approves the final work: the associate removes production time, never judgment.

The work it handles

Research & citations

  • Grounded Minnesota legal research memo from the 630-authority index, for attorney review
  • Citation verification: batch checks against official sources
  • 630 distinct authorities: 30 statutes + 45 rules + 555 cases; 636-row corpus counted 2026-09-27

Drafting (for your review)

  • Demand letters
  • Motions & summary-judgment drafts
  • Complaints, answers & interrogatories
  • Legal memos & settlement agreements

Deadlines & calendaring

  • Minnesota deadline calculations: business days, +3 mail, statutory calendars
  • 17 sourced deadline-rule records; calculation accuracy is not yet certified
  • "What's due?" sweeps that catch nothing late

Case management

  • Case intake into a structured case file
  • PDF extraction, classification & evidence logs
  • Task queues & next-action surfacing

Court operations

  • Courthouse lookups: 87 cards, counted as 88 JSON files less schema
  • E-filing routing guidance (eFS/Tyler, MyCourtMN)
  • Court-form prefill (e.g., CIV117)

Prep & orchestration

  • Deposition & hearing prep briefs
  • Seven-track orchestration on one request
  • Compliance envelope: AI-use disclosure & audit log on every draft

Time & reporting

  • Time record kept from the hours you give it
  • Period time report across a date range
  • Weekly status report: what is due, what moved, what is waiting

Where the hours come from

You tell the associate the time you spent; it keeps the record. It never measures time on its own, and nothing it produces is a billing statement.
