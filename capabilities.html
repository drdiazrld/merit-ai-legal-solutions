Capabilities

Eight working systems. One agent.

The systems below describe the current build. The citation gate is implemented and tested separately; the desk does not call it yet, and that wiring remains in flight under ACC-01.

System A · Research

Full-matter research in minutes

Research pulls from a 630-authority Minnesota and federal index: 30 statutes, 45 court rules, and 555 cases. In one stage run, intake took 3.1 minutes (185 seconds) and missed a deadline; that miss is why the number is still moving. Receipt: measured 2026-09-27 on tree 1b6ddab, n=1 stage run, attorney review time excluded.

  • Statutes, rules, and real cases, organized around your facts
  • Measured intake: 3.1 minutes (185 seconds); that run missed a deadline
  • Outside the index, it says NOT_FOUND. It doesn't guess
The law library
System B · Citation safety

A gate that refuses to invent

The standalone gate returns VERIFIED, NOT_RETRIEVABLE, or AMBIGUOUS. When it runs, it blocks fabricated and overbroad citations. The desk does not call the gate yet; ACC-01 wiring is in flight. A ten-citation gate batch took 21.8 seconds. Receipt: measured 2026-09-27 on tree 1b6ddab, 10 model calls in one batch, attorney review time excluded.

  • Live re-fetch at verification time, not "looks right"
  • Three verdicts: VERIFIED · NOT_RETRIEVABLE · AMBIGUOUS
  • Two cases sharing a page cite? It says AMBIGUOUS and asks you to choose
Checked against the shelf
System C · Drafting

Memos, motions, and filings in minutes

Drafting works from the record, not from memory: research memos, demand letters, motions, complaints, answers, interrogatories, and briefs, in the formats Minnesota courts expect. Drafts preserve citation references for attorney review; the standalone gate can return verdicts when run separately while desk wiring remains in progress.

  • Memos · demands · motions · complaints · answers · interrogatories · briefs
  • Standalone citation verdicts available when the gate is run separately
  • Nothing submits itself. Drafts carry SUBMIT: None
From the record
System D · Case Brain

Every matter, one living file

Case Brain keeps parties, timeline, facts, evidence log, issues, and open tasks in one place, current as the matter grows. Evidence is logged with its source: nothing is asserted into the record without a source behind it.

  • Parties · timeline · facts · evidence · issues · tasks
  • Every assertion carries a source
  • Current as the matter grows, not a static folder
One file per matter
System E · Deadline engine

Seventeen sourced Minnesota deadline rules

The deadline store contains 17 rules, each with its rule number and a live Minnesota Revisor URL. That source count does not certify the calendar math: a 2026-09-27 stage run took 2.1 minutes (125 seconds) and returned 2032 instead of 2028 for Minn. Stat. § 541.07.

  • 17 sourced rule records; calculation accuracy is not yet certified
  • Computed from your matter's dates, not a static calendar
  • Deadlines roll into your matter file automatically
What's due, and when
System F · Court operations

The courthouse, digitized

Court operations keeps 87 courthouse cards for Minnesota courts, covering locations, filing systems, and procedures. Receipt: 88 JSON files counted, comprising 87 cards plus schema.json. It prefills court forms like the CIV117 from your matter record. Filing goes through the courts' own e-filing portal: the agent prepares, the court's system files, you approve every step.

  • 87 courthouse cards (88 JSON files less schema.json)
  • Form prefill: CIV117 and more
  • Filing through the courts' own e-filing portal
87 cards; 88 JSON files less schema.
System G · Orchestration

Seven tracks, one brief

One request is designed to dispatch seven parallel workstreams: facts, research, evidence, weaknesses, opposing arguments, and open tasks, followed by adversarial QA. The prior unmeasured timing estimate has been withdrawn.

  • Seven parallel tracks from a single request
  • Adversarial QA challenges the merged draft
  • One brief in, not seven documents to reconcile
Seven tracks. One brief.
System H · Trust & privacy

Your files stay yours

One agent per attorney, isolated per matter. Only task-relevant content is used for each step, and your files are not used for training. Every action is logged; nothing auto-files or auto-submits, and attorney approval is the last step of every workflow.

  • Per-attorney isolation; task-relevant content only
  • No training on your files
  • Audit log of every action · approval before anything ships
