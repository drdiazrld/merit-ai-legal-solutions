Professional-duty considerations

Minnesota attorneys evaluating AI-assisted preparation may need to consider duties involving competence, confidentiality, candor, and supervision.

Merit does not provide a compliance opinion or claim that use of the product satisfies those duties. Each attorney must evaluate the tool, matter, firm policies, and applicable requirements.

Legal sources and boundaries

Research is prepared with identified sources for attorney review. The authorities that matter depend on the question, jurisdiction, facts, procedure, and current law. Attorneys must open, confirm, update, and evaluate every authority before relying on it.

When a Minnesota statute or rule is referenced, check the current official text through the Minnesota Revisor of Statutes. No source list is represented as complete. Merit does not claim that using the AI Legal Associate automatically satisfies professional duties.

Review before use

  • Compare factual statements with the matter record
  • Read and update cited law
  • Confirm source support and application
  • Calculate deadlines independently
  • Revise every document as needed
  • Complete filing, service, delivery, and communication manually

Workflow limits

Citation checking is separate from drafting and is not claimed on every workflow. A source check does not replace review of existence, currency, treatment, proposition support, or application.

Every proposed deadline requires independent attorney verification. Filing documents remain drafts until the attorney reviews and manually submits them.
