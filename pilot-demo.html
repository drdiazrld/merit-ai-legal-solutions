One matter, end to end: see what the AI Legal Associate actually produces

A fictional Minnesota matter, Johnson v. Smith, run through the product's own pipeline: intake, service and the answer clock, discovery, research with verified citations, and filing prep, every deliverable under attorney review. The documents below are real output from that run, verbatim.

The run

Six legs of a real practice, in one sitting

The matter began with the honest zero-state every new file starts from: no case number, no deadlines on the calendar, no invented record. Building the record was the demonstration.

Intake & engagement

A status question is answered as a pure read, nothing written. The matter record is rebuilt with verified authority attached, and the engagement terms go on file in writing.

Service & the answer clock

The service date is anchored, and the calendar rolls the answer deadline 21 days out, Minn. R. Civ. P. 12.01, with reminders at T-30/14/7/3/1 and the day of.

Discovery

Interrogatories come back flagged draft for review, not served. Deposition prep runs seven parallel tracks: facts, research, evidence, weaknesses, opposing, tasks, questions.

Research

A research memo with verified Minnesota citations, an AI-use disclosure, and honest empty fields, case number and next steps left for the attorney, never invented.

Citation verification

A ten-citation batch checked in seconds: VERIFIED only when the source is on record. Unverified law is refused or routed onward, never presented as real.

Filing prep

The packet is checked against the county's requirements and ends READY_FOR_ATTORNEY_REVIEW. Nothing sends, serves, or files without the attorney. The agent never auto-submits.

The gate

Attorney review is the last step, built in, not promised

Nothing ships without you

Every attorney-facing deliverable ends ready for your review. Drafts are flagged as drafts; filing prep reports SUBMIT: None. The associate prepares. You decide.

Honest where it counts

Citations carry a VERIFIED badge only when the source is on record. Empty fields stay empty for the attorney to fill. Refusing to invent is the feature.

Yours alone

One agent per attorney, isolated per matter. Every action is logged. Your files are not used for training.
