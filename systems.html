The build

Twelve Merit systems. Every one of them ours, every one of them named.

Nothing here is rented from someone else's platform, because there is no platform. Every part below is Merit's own work, and we named every part so that you can ask about any of them directly.

Custody

The only one of these where you hold the key to your own client files.

Others hold your matter content readable on their systems. Ours is sealed: one key per matter, opened by your own passphrase, unreadable to us. That is not a setting we offer. It is how the associate was built.

Merit Vault

Client matter data sealed at rest, one key per matter, opened by the attorney's own passphrase, with recovery codes held by the attorney.

Merit Connect

The connection route. Each connection is authorised by signed instruction from the firm, every read is recorded, revocation is confirmed, and nothing reached through it is copied, stored beyond the session, or resold.

Merit Index

The authority index Merit owns and maintains, built from official and public sources, so research stays inside the building.

Merit Search

Ranked search with exact, phrase, and fielded queries, filters, and the matching words shown, so a result can be judged without opening every document behind it.

Merit Signal

The automated treatment signal, labelled wherever it appears and never presented as a citator.

Merit Library

Model jury instructions, court forms, public filings, and Merit's own issue summaries, holding digests and topic tags.

Merit Casefile

The assembled evidence set, complete before any analysis service is called.

Merit Verify

Citation verification that must resolve and must support the proposition attached to it, or the authority is refused.

Merit Reader

The reader that handles scans and images on the attorney's own seat, so a document does not have to travel to be read.

Merit Seat

One dedicated environment per attorney, with matter boundaries refused when they do not match.

Merit Care

Updates through acceptance checks, support around the clock, and restoration from backup exercised rather than assumed.

Merit Desk

The workspace the attorney works in, in five sections: Training, Associate, Tools, Board, and Connections.
